Why Here

Technique first. Then whatever music you love.

Most players quit not because piano is hard, but because they were never taught how to practise it. Lessons here are built around three things: correct technique, honest listening, and repertoire you'll actually want to play.

01 — FOUNDATION

Technique that transfers

Posture, hand shape, sight-reading and ear training taught in an order that actually compounds — not random songs strung together.

02 — REPERTOIRE

Music you chose, not just the syllabus

Once the fundamentals are solid, lessons pull from film scores, classical grades, and whatever genre pulled you toward piano in the first place.

03 — FEEDBACK LOOP

Short videos between lessons

Regular short-form videos on technique, practice hacks and common mistakes — so the learning doesn't stop when the lesson ends.
